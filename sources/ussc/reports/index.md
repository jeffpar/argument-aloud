# U.S. Reports

## Dates of Decisions and Arguments

The Court's <a href="https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/USReports.aspx" target="_blank">U.S. Reports</a> page also mentions a document entitled "Dates of Supreme Court Decisions and Arguments":

> Explanatory material on the dates of early Supreme Court decisions and arguments from 1791-1882 is available <a href="https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/datesofdecisions.pdf" target="_blank">here</a>.

However, that brief description gives the misleading impression that the document covers *all* decisions and arguments during that period, whereas in fact, it covers only decisions and arguments in that period that were *also* reported in Volumes 2 through 107 of U.S. Reports. As we discussed in our blog post "[Working on the Weekends](/courts/ussc/?link=/courts/ussc/blog/2026/working-on-the-weekends#cases-missing-from-dates-of-decisions)", Volumes 131 and 154 also reported cases from that period which are *not* included in the above document.

And, in typical fashion, the Court updated the document in 2018 without mentioning *what* was updated -- aside from helpfully mentioning that it was "Updated December 26, 2018".

As far as we can tell, the only change was the argument date for *Marbury v. Madison (5 U.S. 137)*, from "Feb. 11, 1803" to "Feb. 10-11, 1803".
