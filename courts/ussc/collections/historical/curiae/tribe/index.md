# Curiae: Tribe

> Laurence H. Tribe, *American Constitutional Law*, vol. 1 (3d ed., Foundation Press, 2000)

The most influential modern single-author treatise on U.S. constitutional law, by Harvard professor Laurence H. Tribe. Only volume 1 of the third edition was ever published.

A **Curiae: Tribe** tag marks every case that this work singled out for *primary treatment*, as determined by Yale Law School’s now-defunct [Curiae Project](/courts/ussc/?collection=popular&id=all).
