# Curiae: Landmark Briefs

> *Landmark Briefs and Arguments of the Supreme Court of the United States: Constitutional Law*, compiled by Philip B. Kurland & Gerhard Casper (University Publications of America)

A roughly 80-volume set that reprints the party briefs and oral-argument transcripts of constitutionally significant cases from 1793 forward, with a supplement volume for each later Term. Editors Kurland and Casper were University of Chicago law professors. (The Curiae page lists the editors as “Gerald Gunther & Gerhard Casper”; the set is conventionally attributed to Kurland & Casper.)

A **Curiae: Landmark Briefs** tag marks a case that this work singles out for *primary treatment* — a separate index or case-list entry — in the survey behind the Yale Law School [Curiae Project](/courts/ussc/?collection=popular&id=all).
