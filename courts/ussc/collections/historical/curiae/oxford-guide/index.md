# Curiae: Oxford Guide

> *The Oxford Guide to United States Supreme Court Decisions*, Kermit L. Hall, ed. (Oxford Univ. Press, 1999)

A single-volume guide with concise entries on roughly 400 of the Court’s most important decisions, drawn from *The Oxford Companion to the Supreme Court of the United States*.

A **Curiae: Oxford Guide** tag marks every case that this work singled out for *primary treatment*, as determined by Yale Law School’s now-defunct [Curiae Project](/courts/ussc/?collection=popular&id=all).
