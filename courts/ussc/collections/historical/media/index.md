# Media Files Cited in Opinions

As the U.S. Supreme Court's [website](https://www.supremecourt.gov/media/media.aspx) explains:

> On occasion, an opinion may cite to a media file, e.g., a video or audio file that is part of the record in the lower court. Those files are posted here.

More importantly, unlike the Court's website, any files associated with these cases are always listed *with* the case, regardless of any other collection(s) in which they may also appear.  So, even when browsing a term like [October Term 2020](/courts/ussc/?term=2020-10), a case such as [Edwards v. Vannoy (No. 19-5807)](/courts/ussc/?term=2020-10&case=19-5807) will always list any related files, including any media files.
