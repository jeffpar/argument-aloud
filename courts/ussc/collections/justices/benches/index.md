<div id="jb-intro" markdown="1">

Every time a new Justice joins or an existing Justice leaves, a new **Bench** is formed.  Bench names use the last name of the Chief Justice and are numbered in chronological order.  Some may refer to these groups as Courts, but we prefer the term Bench, since Court is such an overloaded term.  For example, when people refer to the "Roberts Court", they might be referring to the Justices currently sitting with [John Roberts](/courts/ussc/?collection=gallery&id=john_roberts) *or* to his entire tenure as Chief Justice.

We define a new Bench whenever *any* change in membership occurs that is *also* followed by one or more decisions before another change in membership occurs.  This means that if a Justice joined and then left before any other Justice joined or left, returning the Court to its previous membership, that would still result in a *new* Bench.  So far, that has happened only once, in 1828, when Justice [Robert Trimble](/courts/ussc/?collection=gallery&id=robert_trimble) died, resulting in a [Marshall 11](/courts/ussc/?collection=benches&id=marshall11) bench that was identical to the [Marshall 9](/courts/ussc/?collection=benches&id=marshall9) bench.
