[The Oyez Project](https://www.oyez.org/advocates/paul_d_clement) has a lower total because it's missing arguments. For example, he argued in all *three* of the consolidated cases in [National Federation of Independent Business v. Sebelius (2012)](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=paul_d_clement&term=2011-10&case=11-398), which Oyez lists only once.

Our data still needs to be reviewed as well, because after [United States Forest Service v. Cowpasture River Preservation Ass'n (No. 18-1584)](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=paul_d_clement&term=2019-10&case=18-1584&event=2) was argued, the Chief Justice noted that was Mr. Clements's 100th argument; however, we have it listed as his 99th.

Similarly, it was reported on October 16, 2014 (in a now-unavailable [document](http://www.bancroftpllc.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/2014-10-16-NLJ-Clement-Marks-Milestone-of-75-Supreme-Court-Arguments-1.pdf)) that his 75th argument was [Integrity Staffing Solutions, Inc. v. Busk](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=paul_d_clement&term=2014-10&case=13-433&event=2), but we list that argument as his 74th.

The missing argument presumably occurred sometime between October 2009 and October 2014, because all [indications](https://web.archive.org/web/20100710122236/https://legaltimes.typepad.com/blt/2009/10/paul-clement-43-celebrates-his-50th-argument.html) are that his 50th argument was [Perdue v. Kenny A](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=paul_d_clement&term=2009-10&case=08-970&event=2).
