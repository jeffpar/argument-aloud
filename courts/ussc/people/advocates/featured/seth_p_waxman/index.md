# Seth P. Waxman

As of this writing (May 2026), we are aware of a total of 88 arguments by Mr. Waxman.

While our total agrees with [The Oyez Project](https://www.oyez.org/advocates/seth_p_waxman), it is purely by accident, because their list includes [Devlin v. Scardelletti (No. 01-417)](/courts/ussc/?term=2001-10&case=01-417), in which Mr. Waxman only particpated in the briefs, not oral argument, and it fails to list all his arguments in [Whitman v. American Trucking Associations, Inc. (No. 99-1257)](/courts/ussc/?term=2000-10&case=99-1257&event=3), where he argued separately in the consolidated case [American Trucking Associations, Inc. v. Whitman (No. 99-1426)](/courts/ussc/?term=2000-10&case=99-1257&event=4).
