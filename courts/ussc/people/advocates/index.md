# Advocates

These are people who have argued cases at the U.S. Supreme Court.

NOTE: This list is not comprehensive. It is limited to what the Court sometimes refers to as the "modern era," although it has never really established what dates that era encompasses.

This list should include all advocates who argued since October Term 1955, as well as a few featured advocates such as [Thurgood Marshall](/courts/ussc/?collection=justice_advocates&id=thurgood_marshall), who first argued in 1943, as well as all known [Women Advocates](/courts/ussc/?collection=women_advocates), the earliest of which was [Belva Ann Lockwood](/courts/ussc/?collection=women_advocates&id=belva_ann_lockwood&term=1880-10&case=90), who first argued in 1880.
