# Top 100 Advocates

Of all people who have argued at the U.S. Supreme Court over the last 70 years, the current record holder is [Edwin Kneedler](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=edwin_s_kneedler), who argued 160 times at the Court.

To go back more than 70 years, complete and accurate data is harder to come by.  For now, we refer you to a recent report on [Oyez](https://www.oyez.org/top-advocates); we're in the process of tracking down all the underlying data.

In that report, the following list of "Top Advocates" was provided.  Note that their totals were as of February 4, 2022, which will obviously be out-of-date for any attorneys who have argued since then.
