# Top 100 Advocates

Of all people who have argued at the U.S. Supreme Court over the last 70 years, the current record holder is [Edwin Kneedler](/courts/ussc/?collection=top_advocates&id=edwin_s_kneedler), who argued 160 times at the Court.

To go back more than 70 years, accurate data is harder to come by.  For now, we refer you to a recent report on [Oyez](https://www.oyez.org/top-advocates).  The author, James M. Thunder, planned to upload a spreadsheet to Google Drive, but so far, we've been unable to track it down.
