<p>Born October 13, 1924 in Fort Dodge, Iowa.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=stafford3">May 6, 1976</a> to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=durham3">January 11, 1999</a> (22.7 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 14, 1985 to January 10, 1987.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Evans (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p>Voted in 18 cases decided from June 24, 1982 to December 24, 1998 (15 in the majority, 3 in dissent).</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Swarthmore, B.A. (1949)<br>Univeristy of Washington, J.D. (1952)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Administrative Assistant to Member of Congress (1954-1960)<br>Administrative Assistant to Governor (1964-1976)</p>
