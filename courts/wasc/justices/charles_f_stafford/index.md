<p>Born June 24, 1918 in Burlington, Washington. Died July 3, 1984.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=hunter2">January 1, 1970</a> to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=williams1">July 3, 1984</a> (14.5 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 13, 1975 to January 10, 1977.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Evans (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p>Voted in 2 cases decided from June 24, 1982 to January 26, 1984 (1 in the majority, 1 in dissent).</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Whitman College, B.A. (1940)<br>Yale University, LL.B. (1946)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Deputy Prosecutor (1947-1953)<br>Superior Court (1953-1969)<br>Court of Appeals (1969)</p>
