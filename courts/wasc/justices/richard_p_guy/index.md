<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born October 24, 1932 in Coeur d'Alene, Idaho.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=callow2">November 1, 1989</a> to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=guy1">January 8, 2001</a> (11.2 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from September 30, 1999 to January 8, 2001.</p>
<p>Party: Democrat.</p>
<p>Voted in 20 cases decided from May 9, 1996 to September 28, 2000 (17 in the majority, 3 in dissent).</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Gonzaga University (1957)<br>Gonzaga Law School, J.D. (1959)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Law Clerk (1957-1959)<br>Assistant Attorney General (1959-1961)<br>Attorney for Washington Speaker of House of Representatives (1963)<br>Deputy Prosecutor (1961-1964)<br>Superior Court (1977-1981; 1985)</p>
