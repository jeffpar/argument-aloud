<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born December 31, 1867 in Haubstadt, Indiana. Died September 13, 1948.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=morris1">January 11, 1915</a> to January 10, 1927 and from April 22, 1927 to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=steinert2">January 9, 1939</a> (23.7 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from June 1, 1919 to January 10, 1921.</p>
<p>Party: Democrat.</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Southwestern Indiana Normal School (1888)<br>Chicago College of Law, LL.B. (1892)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Prosecuting Attorney (1895-1898)<br>State Commissioner of Arid Lands (1898-1901)<br>Ritzville Mayor and City Councilman (1899-1908)<br>Superior Court (1909-1915)</p>
