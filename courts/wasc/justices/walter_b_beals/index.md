<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born July 21, 1876 in St. Paul, Minnesota. Died September 18, 1960.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=mackintosh3">April 16, 1928</a> to October 1, 1946 and from September 10, 1947 to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=schwellenbach2">September 10, 1951</a> (22.5 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 9, 1933 to January 14, 1935.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Hartley (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>University of Washington School of Law, LL.B. (1901)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>U.S. Army (1917-1919; 1946-1947)<br>Seattle Corporate Counsel (1923-1926)<br>Superior Court (1926-1928)</p>
