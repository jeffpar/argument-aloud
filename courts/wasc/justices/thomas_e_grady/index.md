<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born November 19, 1880 in Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin. Died April 5, 1974.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=robinson2">November 25, 1942</a> to November 30, 1945 and from January 10, 1949 to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=grady1">January 10, 1955</a> (9 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 12, 1953 to January 10, 1955.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Langlie (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>St. Paul College of Law (1901-1902)<br>University of Minnesota, LL.B. (1904)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Deputy Prosecuting Attorney (1905-1906)<br>Superior Court (1911-1917)<br>Yakima City Attorney (1917-1923)<br>President, Washington State Bar Association (1939-1940)</p>
