<p>Chief Justice from December 18, 1945 to April 22, 1946.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Martin (Democrat).</p>
<p>Party: Democrat.</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>University of Washington, LL.B. (1916)<br>Georgetown University, LL.M. (1926)</p>
