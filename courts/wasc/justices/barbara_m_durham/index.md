<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born October 6, 1942 in Anacortes, Washington. Died December 30, 2002.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=dolliver1">January 14, 1985</a> to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=durham4">September 30, 1999</a> (14.7 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 9, 1995 to September 30, 1999.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Spellman (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p>Voted in 19 cases decided from July 18, 1985 to July 1, 1999 (15 in the majority, 4 in dissent).</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Gonzaga University (1961)<br>Georgetown University, B.A. (1964)<br>Stanford University, J.D. (1968)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Deputy Prosecutor (1968-1970)<br>District Court (1973-1976)<br>Superior Court (1977-1980)<br>Court of Appeals (1980-1984)</p>
