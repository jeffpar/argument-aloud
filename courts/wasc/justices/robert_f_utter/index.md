<p>Appointed by Governor Evans (Republican).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p>Voted in 5 cases decided from June 24, 1982 to May 14, 2009 (3 in the majority, 2 in dissent).</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Linfield College (1948-1950)<br>University of Washington, B.S. (1952)<br>University of Washington, LL.B. (1954)</p>
