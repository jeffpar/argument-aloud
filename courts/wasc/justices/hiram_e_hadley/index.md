<h1>{{ page.title }}</h1>
<p>Born January 16, 1854 in Sylvania, Indiana. Died January 13, 1929.</p>
<p>Served from <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=reavis2">March 20, 1901</a> to October 7, 1902 and from January 12, 1903 to <a href="/courts/wasc/?collection=benches&id=hadley2">January 11, 1909</a> (7.6 years).</p>
<p>Chief Justice from January 14, 1907 to January 11, 1909.</p>
<p>Appointed by Governor Rogers (Democrat).</p>
<p>Party: Republican.</p>
<p><b>Education:</b><br>Earlham College (1872-1874)<br>Union College of Law, B.L. (1877)</p>
<p><b>Career:</b><br>Bellingham City Attorney (1891-1896)<br>Superior Court (1896-1900)<br>President, Seattle Bar Association (1915)</p>
